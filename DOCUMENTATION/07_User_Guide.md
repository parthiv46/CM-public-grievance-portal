# User guide

## Citizen

Create an account or use the Citizen demo login. Choose **Submit a grievance**, select the district and category, enter a subject and at least 20 characters of detail, and optionally add a small image or document. AI suggestions are optional and editable. After submission, save the generated reference. The dashboard shows submissions, current status, department and update dates. Open a reference for the full timeline, attachments and CMO response. Public tracking can also look up a reference without signing in. After resolution, add feedback or request reopening.

## CMO administrator

Sign in with the CMO admin demo account. The operations dashboard shows totals and filters for district and status. Open a grievance to view its description, attachment and timeline, then select a department and record the review note. Assigned grievances appear in the relevant officer’s workspace. Review an action-taken report when one is submitted, and record a response or return the case for further action.

## Department officer

Sign in with the officer demo account. Only cases assigned to that officer’s demonstration department are listed. Open a case, record investigation remarks and an action-taken report. Selecting **Resolved** sends the report to the CMO for review; the officer cannot directly resolve the case for the citizen.

## Demo safety

Use fictional names, contact details and grievance descriptions. This prototype has no connection to official services, and public reference tracking should be treated as a demonstration feature rather than a production privacy design.
